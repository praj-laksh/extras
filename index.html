    FEATURED PROJECT / 2025

    Multi-Factor Equity Signal Research & Portfolio Optimization

    THE QUESTION

    Do value, momentum and quality factors help explain why some stocks earn higher returns than others, and can they be combined into a portfolio that holds up out of sample?

    APPROACH

    1. Signal engineering. Built predictive factor signals from historical datasets, ranking stocks cross-sectionally and testing the signals with hypothesis tests.
    2. Model analysis. Applied regression and machine-learning algorithms to study factor interactions, signal stability and performance across market regimes.
    3. Portfolio evaluation. Programmed a walk-forward backtest with optimization constraints and risk controls, so performance is measured only on data the model hadn’t seen.

    TOOLS

    PythonPandasNumPySciPyStatsmodelsScikit-learnFactor modelsBacktesting
