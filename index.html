    Quantitative research, Mar 2025

    Multi-factor equity signal research & portfolio optimization

    The question

    Do value, momentum and quality factors help explain why some stocks earn higher returns than others, and can they be combined into a portfolio that holds up out of sample?

    Approach

    1. Signal engineering. Built predictive factor signals from historical datasets, ranking stocks cross-sectionally on each factor and testing the signals with hypothesis tests.
    2. Model analysis. Applied regression and machine-learning algorithms to study how the factors interact, how stable each signal is, and how performance changes across market regimes.
    3. Portfolio evaluation. Programmed a walk-forward backtest with optimization constraints and risk controls, so performance is measured only on data the model hadn’t seen.

    Tools

    Python (Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Statsmodels, Scikit-learn), factor models, regression analysis, hypothesis testing, portfolio optimization and backtesting.
